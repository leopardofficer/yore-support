## Frequently Asked Questions · 常见问题

### Why are some photos missing?
### 为什么部分照片没有显示？

Yore can only display photos you have allowed it to access. In iPhone Settings, find Yore and review its Photos permissions. You can allow access to more selected photos or your full library.

Yore 只能显示你授权访问的照片。请在 iPhone「设置」中找到 Yore，检查照片访问权限。你可以增加允许访问的照片，或授权访问完整图库。

### How do I add a Home Screen widget?
### 如何添加桌面小组件？

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, open the widget gallery, search for Yore, and choose a widget size. If the widget offers configuration options, touch and hold it and select Edit Widget.

长按主屏幕空白处，打开小组件列表，搜索 Yore 并选择尺寸。添加后，如果小组件支持配置，可长按它并选择「编辑小组件」。

### How do I restore my purchases?
### 如何恢复购买？

Sign in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase, then open the Yore Plus purchase screen and tap Restore Purchases. If your purchase is still unavailable, contact us.

请使用购买时的 Apple 账户登录，然后在 Yore Plus 购买页面点击「恢复购买」。如果仍无法恢复，请通过邮箱联系我们。

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
### 如何管理或取消订阅？

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Yore to manage or cancel your subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

打开 iPhone「设置」→ 你的姓名 →「订阅」→ Yore，即可管理或取消订阅。删除 App 不会自动取消订阅。
