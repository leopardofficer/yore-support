Yore 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 16 日

简体中文 · English · 技术支持 / Support

1. 关于本政策

Yore 由丁燕玲（以下简称“我们”）提供。 本政策说明你使用 Yore 及其支持网站时，相关信息如何被访问、 使用、存储及共享，以及你可以如何管理这些信息。

Yore 无需注册独立账户。照片浏览、回忆整理、小组件配置及 照片分析主要在你的设备上完成。我们不运营用于接收你的 相册、笔记或保险库内容的服务器。

2. 照片、视频及本地数据

获得你的授权后，Yore 会访问你允许使用的照片、Live Photos、 视频及相关元数据，例如媒体标识、拍摄日期、尺寸、时长和 照片中已有的位置信息。这些信息用于展示回忆、日期筛选、 相册统计、相似内容分组及你选择使用的其他照片功能。

你添加的笔记、喜欢标记、纪念日、分组名称、小组件配置、 缩略图及分析缓存保存在设备上的应用存储空间中。 为显示小组件，你选定的照片副本和配置可能保存在 Yore 与其小组件共用的本地存储空间中。 小组件中的内容会显示在你放置它的系统界面上。

当你使用人物、宠物或相似照片分组功能时，Yore 可能在设备上 检测面部或其他图像区域，生成用于比较的特征数据，并保存 分组结果及缩略图。这些数据用于整理你的照片， 不用于身份验证、广告画像或训练我们的机器学习模型， 也不会由 Yore 上传给我们或第三方分析服务。

3. 权限及照片位置信息

  • 照片权限： 用于浏览、分析和导入你授权的媒体，以及执行你主动选择的 保存或删除操作。你可以选择仅授权部分照片。
  • 相机权限： 用于你主动在保险库中拍摄照片或视频。
  • 麦克风权限： 用于为你在保险库中拍摄的视频录制声音。

Yore 使用的是照片中已有的拍摄位置，不持续追踪你的实时位置。 当照片详情或统计页面需要显示地点名称且网络可用时， Yore 可能通过 Apple 的地图或地理编码服务，将相关照片的 经纬度转换为地址或地点名称。该过程可能将坐标和必要的 请求信息发送给 Apple 服务，并在本地缓存返回结果。

你可以在 iPhone“设置”中调整或撤回照片、相机和麦克风权限。 撤回权限会影响相关功能，但不会自动删除此前已保存的 小组件照片、保险库副本或本地记录。

4. 图片保险库

导入或拍摄到保险库中的照片和视频以加密文件形式保存在设备上。 解锁所需的密钥材料受系统钥匙串保护。 文件夹名称、媒体类型和时间等索引信息使用系统文件保护。 浏览、播放或分享时，应用可能临时生成解密内容， 并在相关操作结束或保险库锁定后清理临时文件。

保险库内容不上传到我们的服务器，保险库目录被排除在系统备份之外。 我们无法替你找回保险库密码或恢复其内容。 忘记密码或删除 App 可能导致保险库内容永久丢失。

导入保险库不代表原件必然从系统相册消失。 只有你选择删除原件并完成系统确认后，应用才执行相应删除操作。 系统相册中的“最近删除”和 iCloud 同步规则仍然适用。 你主动导出或分享的副本由目标应用、接收方或存储服务管理。

5. Apple 服务、购买及第三方处理

如果你使用 iCloud 照片，Yore 可能通过 Apple 的照片框架 下载显示、播放或导入所需的媒体。该过程由 Apple 的系统服务处理。 保险库以外的部分本地应用数据可能受你设备的备份设置影响。

Yore Plus 的购买和恢复购买通过 Apple App Store 与 StoreKit 完成。 Yore 会读取必要的交易和订阅状态，例如产品、有效期及撤销状态， 用于判断功能使用资格。我们不会收到你的银行卡号或完整支付信息。

Yore 未集成第三方广告或用户行为分析 SDK，不进行跨应用广告追踪， 也不出售或出租你的个人信息。你主动使用系统分享功能时， 所选内容会交给你指定的应用或服务。

Apple 对其服务的数据处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。 其他由你选择使用的分享或存储服务适用各自的隐私政策。

6. 联系支持与访问网站

当你通过邮件联系我们时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址，以及 你主动提供的问题描述、设备信息、截图或附件。 我们使用这些信息回复请求、排查问题和改进相关功能。 邮件服务提供商也会为传递和存储邮件处理这些信息。 请避免发送与问题无关的私人照片、密码或完整支付资料。

本支持网站托管于 GitHub Pages。GitHub 会出于安全目的记录 网站访问者的 IP 地址。详情请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明 。 本页面未添加广告、第三方分析脚本或追踪 Cookie。 如果你在公开的 GitHub Issues 中反馈问题， 其中的内容可能被任何人查看。

7. 保存期限、删除与选择

本地内容通常保留至你使用应用内相应功能删除、 应用清理缓存或删除 App 时。分析特征和统计缓存可能保留， 以避免重复计算；撤回照片权限不会自动清除这些历史缓存。 删除 App 可移除其本地应用数据，但不会自动删除系统相册原件、 已导出的副本、外部备份或 Apple 的购买记录。 系统钥匙串及备份中的信息由 iOS 的相应机制管理。

支持邮件仅在处理请求、必要的后续沟通和履行适用法律义务所需的 期间保留。你可以通过下方邮箱请求访问、更正或删除我们持有的 支持通信中的个人信息，也可以就适用的数据权利联系我们。 对于仅保存在你设备上的内容，我们无法远程读取或删除。

你可以随时调整系统权限或停止使用相关功能。 删除 App 不会自动取消订阅；订阅可在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理。

8. 安全及政策更新

我们使用系统文件保护、保险库加密和访问控制等措施保护信息， 但任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。 请妥善保管设备及保险库密码。

当功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策， 并修改页面顶部的更新日期。对于重大变化， 我们会根据适用要求提供进一步提示或取得必要同意。

Yore Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. About this policy

Yore is provided by 丁燕玲 (Ding Yanling, “we”). This policy explains how information is accessed, used, stored, and shared when you use Yore and its support website, and how you can manage that information.

Yore does not require a separate account. Photo browsing, memory organization, widget configuration, and photo analysis primarily take place on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your photo library, notes, or vault contents.

2. Photos, videos, and local data

With your permission, Yore accesses the photos, Live Photos, videos, and associated metadata you authorize, such as media identifiers, capture dates, dimensions, duration, and existing photo location information. This information supports memories, date filtering, library statistics, similar-photo grouping, and other photo features you choose to use.

Notes, likes, anniversary dates, group names, widget settings, thumbnails, and analysis caches are stored locally. Copies of selected widget photos and their settings may be stored in a local container shared by Yore and its widget extensions. Widget contents are visible on the system surfaces where you place the widgets.

When you use people, pet, or similar-photo grouping, Yore may detect faces or other image regions on your device, generate comparison features, and store grouping results and thumbnails. This information is used to organize photos, not for identity authentication, advertising profiles, or training our machine learning models. Yore does not upload it to us or to third-party analytics services.

3. Permissions and photo locations

  • Photos: To browse, analyze, and import authorized media, and perform save or delete actions you choose. You may grant access to selected photos only.
  • Camera: To capture photos or videos when you use the vault camera.
  • Microphone: To record sound with videos captured in the vault.

Yore uses location metadata already attached to photos; it does not continuously track your live location. When photo details or statistics need place names and network access is available, Yore may use Apple's mapping or geocoding services to convert photo coordinates into addresses or place names. Coordinates and necessary request information may be sent to Apple services, and results may be cached locally.

You can change or revoke Photos, Camera, and Microphone permissions in iPhone Settings. Revoking permission affects related features but does not automatically delete previously saved widget photos, vault copies, or local records.

4. Private vault

Photos and videos imported or captured into the vault are stored as encrypted files on your device. Key material used to unlock the vault is protected by the system Keychain. Index information, such as folder names, media types, and dates, uses system file protection. Viewing, playback, or sharing may create temporary decrypted content, which is cleaned up after the relevant operation or when the vault locks.

Vault contents are not uploaded to our servers, and the vault directory is excluded from system backups. We cannot recover your vault passcode or contents. Forgetting your passcode or deleting the app may permanently destroy access to vault media.

Importing media does not necessarily remove the originals from Photos. Deletion occurs only when you choose to delete originals and complete the system confirmation. Apple's Recently Deleted and iCloud synchronization rules still apply. Copies you export or share are managed by the destination app, recipient, or storage service.

5. Apple services, purchases, and third parties

If you use iCloud Photos, Yore may download media through Apple's Photos framework for viewing, playback, or import. These transfers are handled by Apple system services. Some local app data outside the vault may be included in backups depending on your device settings.

Yore Plus purchases and restoration use the Apple App Store and StoreKit. Yore reads necessary transaction and subscription information, such as product, expiration, and revocation status, to determine feature access. We do not receive your card number or full payment details.

Yore does not include third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs, perform cross-app advertising tracking, or sell or rent your personal information. When you use system sharing, selected content is passed to the app or service you choose.

Apple's handling of its services is described in the Apple Privacy Policy. Other sharing or storage services you choose have their own privacy policies.

6. Support communications and website visits

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the descriptions, device details, screenshots, or attachments you choose to provide. We use this information to respond, investigate problems, and improve related features. Email providers also process this information to deliver and store messages. Please avoid sending unrelated private photos, passwords, or full payment information.

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement . This page does not add advertisements, third-party analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. Information you post in public GitHub Issues may be visible to anyone.

7. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local content is generally retained until you remove it using available app controls, the app clears cached content, or you delete the app. Analysis features and statistical caches may remain to avoid repeated processing; revoking photo access does not automatically erase these historical caches. Deleting the app removes its local app data, but does not automatically delete originals in Photos, exported copies, external backups, or Apple's purchase records. Keychain and backup information are managed by iOS mechanisms.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle requests, necessary follow-up, and applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold in support correspondence, or to exercise applicable data rights. We cannot remotely access or delete content stored only on your device.

You may change system permissions or stop using related features at any time. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.

8. Security and policy changes

We use measures including system file protection, vault encryption, and access controls to protect information. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Please protect your device and vault passcode.

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. We will revise the date shown above and provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes where required.
